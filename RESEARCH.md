## 6. Competitors — designer cake bakers in Rohini / North Delhi

### 1. Garhwal Bakers — Rohini (closest local rival WITH a website)
- Site: garhwalbakers.com — "WHERE FRESHNESS MEETS TASTE". Hero video, services: Custom Cakes, Quick Delivery (same-day in Rohini), Fresh Baking; customer testimonial; Contact Us / Order Now buttons.
- Phone +91 70421 66340. Custom cakes, pre-order, bulk-order delivery, takeout + home delivery. Budget-friendly ("inexpensive" price level). Open 10 AM–10 PM.
- **Lesson for our demo:** a simple site with same-day delivery + custom cakes already beats having no site. Piinacle's edge over Garhwal: designer/artisan positioning, eggless-only, hampers, event dessert service.

### 2. L'amour Sugar — Model Town / Gujranwala Town, North Delhi (premium benchmark)
- Site: lamoursugar.com — full e-commerce-style patisserie site. Bespoke eggless cakes since 2016, same-day delivery across Delhi, complimentary tasting on bespoke orders, full menu with prices (Classic Fresh Fruit Cake ₹1,250 serves 4–6; theme cake ₹3,950 serves 10–15; cookie tin ₹1,799; slice ₹489), WhatsApp enquiry on every product, Diwali range, gift hampers.
- WeddingWire: starting ₹1,500–1,999/kg. Phone +91 99990 95565.
- **Lesson:** the polished end of the market — per-product WhatsApp buttons, prices shown, Diwali gifting section. Piinacle should NOT copy the premium pricing; her advantage is warm, homemade, moderate prices, Rohini-local.

### 3. Baked By Priti — Noida (best home-bakery website model)
- Site: bakedbypriti.in — premium home bakery site: custom cakes, cupcakes, pinata boxes, 100% eggless, founder story ("Hi, I'm Priti — and I bake with heart"), WhatsApp ordering ("share a reference image on WhatsApp"), UPI/bank transfer/COD, FAQ (how to order, eggless, custom designs), testimonials.
- **Lesson:** the exact playbook for Piinacle — founder story + WhatsApp-first ordering + FAQ. Steal the structure, not the copy.

### 4. Bakingo — Delhi (mass-market chain)
- Site: bakingo.com — online chain bakery. Same-day, midnight and 2-hour express delivery; half-kg eggless cakes from ~₹825; jar cakes, cheesecakes; price-led and fast.
- **Lesson:** the commodity alternative. Piinacle's demo must sell the opposite: custom, handmade, personal — not speed/price.

### 5. Crazy Cakes by Ankita — Paschim Vihar (closest direct analogue)
- Instagram-first eggless home baker, 4.9★ (10 reviews). Super-soft multi-layered cakes, minimal cream, custom designs, home delivery. Phone +91 98736 55998.
- **Lesson:** same segment, same rating, also without a full website — proof that the segment's ceiling without a site is "good Instagram + phone orders". A website is the differentiator.

Also nearby: Rich Bake, Rohini (IG @richbakecakes, custom cakes, vegan/gluten-free options, home delivery) and Cake O' Clock, Rohini (chocolate truffle, blueberry, Kitkat cakes, takeout + delivery).
